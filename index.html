    How this is calculated

    Assumptions, time-of-use split, season weights, hourly data, sources

    Each plan is costed month by month on your usage: daily charge × days, plus kWh × the plan's rates (time-of-use plans split by when you use power), plus GST where needed, minus discounts and credits you'd get. Prices come only from retailers' own websites, your bills, or quotes you add. Always confirm with the retailer before switching.

    Low user vs standard user

    Low-user plans have a lower daily charge but a higher price per kWh. Standard-user plans are the reverse. So low-user plans suit homes that use less power, and standard-user plans suit bigger users. The break-even is the yearly kWh where both cost the same. While the regulations apply, retailers have to price the two so they cost about the same at 8,000 kWh a year (Auckland).
