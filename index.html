      Low user vs standard user

      Low-user plans have a lower daily charge but a higher price per kWh. Standard-user plans are the reverse. So low-user plans suit homes that use less power, and standard-user plans suit bigger users. The break-even is the yearly kWh where both cost the same. While the regulations apply, retailers have to price the two so they cost about the same at 8,000 kWh a year (Auckland).

      Disclaimer

      These are estimates only. Real bills depend on your exact address, meter type, the retailer's terms, price changes, seasonal usage and when you use power. Retail rates in this app come only from retailers' own websites (fetched automatically, with the URL and time), your own bills, and quotes you entered from a retailer's site. Always confirm the rates for your address with the retailer before switching.

      Prices in NZ dollars incl. 15% GST unless stated. Built for personal use.
